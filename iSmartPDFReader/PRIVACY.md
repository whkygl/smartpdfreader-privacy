# iSmartPDFReader privacy policy / 隐私政策

Updated / 更新：2026-10-25

## 中文

本节适用于免费开源的 1.2.0 ChatGPT 账号版本，包括其预览版；并不表示该版本已经发布到 App Store。

**本地阅读。** 打开、显示、搜索和标注 PDF 在本机完成。无需登录即可使用阅读和批注。应用不会因打开 PDF 而上传文件。偏好设置保存在本机；保存批注会写入您选择的 PDF 文件。

**可选的 OpenAI 问答。** 首次发送问题前，应用会征求向 OpenAI 共享问答所需内容的许可。您发送问题时，问题、为该问题选取的 PDF 文字、当前选区、附图及当前对话上下文会经 HTTPS 直接发送到 OpenAI。应用可携带文档名称和页码帮助引用。长文档使用本地选取的摘录；应用不会把摘录等同于完整阅读。开发者不运行转发或存储这些内容的服务器。

**登录。** 登录和授权在系统浏览器的 OpenAI 官方页面完成，应用不接收您的账号密码。OpenAI 返回账号标识、电子邮箱和授权凭据；应用将其保存在自己的受保护本地目录，仅用于账号显示、认证和续期。首次注册的随机主机标识会发送给 OpenAI，以识别此安装的授权注册。问答请求与您的 ChatGPT 授权关联。模型、权限和额度由 OpenAI 决定。

**保存和删除。** 阅读窗口内的对话保存在应用内存中，不同步到 ChatGPT 网页历史；新对话、切换文档或退出账号会清空相应对话。应用不提供永久聊天存档。退出账号会删除此应用的本地登录凭据，不会注销或删除您的 ChatGPT 账号，也不会清除其他应用的数据。您可在设置中撤回内容分享许可。撤回或退出不能收回已经提交给 OpenAI 的内容；对服务方保存数据的查看、删除和授权管理，请使用 OpenAI 提供的账号控制。仅把应用移入废纸篓可能不会自动删除 macOS 支持目录中的数据。

**服务方处理。** OpenAI 对所接收内容、账号及必要连接信息的处理受其[隐私政策](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/)、适用条款和您的账号设置约束。本应用不承诺服务方不保存、不训练或立即删除内容。应用请求关闭服务端对话存储，这不等于服务方所有系统均零留存。请仅提交您有权分享的资料，并谨慎处理机密或个人资料。

**无追踪。** 应用没有广告、广告标识、使用分析或行为追踪代码。页灵的鼠标跟随和动画在本地计算，鼠标位置与动画计时不会发送出设备。系统和服务方可能分别按各自政策处理必要网络、安全或商店信息。

**早期版本。** 1.0.x 使用用户自行设置的 Gemini 或 Deepseek API 密钥。使用这些旧版的 AI 功能时，相关文档内容、问题及支持的附图会直接提交至用户选定的服务方，并使用相应密钥进行认证；密钥和偏好保存在本地设置。有关服务方的数据处理请查阅 [Google 隐私政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy) 和 [Deepseek 隐私政策](https://www.deepseek.com/privacy)。本节不应被理解为旧版的密钥只在本机使用而从不发送认证请求。

**联系和变更。** 隐私咨询：whkygl@163.com。政策变更会在此页更新日期。请勿通过公开问题或邮件发送令牌、密码或私人 PDF。

## English

This section covers the free, open-source 1.2.0 ChatGPT account edition and its preview. It does not announce App Store availability.

**Local reading.** PDF viewing, searching and annotations run on your Mac without signing in. Opening a PDF does not upload it. Preferences are local; saving annotations writes to the PDF you select.

**Optional OpenAI Q&A.** Before the first question, the app asks permission to share the content needed for Q&A. Sending a question transmits the question, locally selected PDF excerpts, selections, attached images and required conversation context directly to OpenAI over HTTPS. A document name and page references may be included. Excerpts from long documents are not a complete reading. The developer operates no relay or content-storage server.

**Account access.** Sign-in and authorization happen on OpenAI's official page in your system browser. The app does not receive your password. OpenAI returns an account identifier, email and credentials, which are kept in this app's protected local directory for account display, authentication and renewal. A random installation host identifier is sent for client registration. Requests are associated with your ChatGPT authorization; OpenAI controls account access, models and limits.

**Retention and controls.** Window conversations stay in memory rather than syncing with ChatGPT web history. A new conversation, document change or sign-out clears the corresponding conversation. There is no permanent chat archive. Signing out deletes this app's local credentials and does not delete your ChatGPT account or other apps' data. Content-sharing permission can be withdrawn in Settings. These actions cannot retract content already sent to OpenAI; use OpenAI's account controls for service-side data and authorization management. Moving the app to Trash may leave macOS support-directory data behind.

**Service provider.** OpenAI handles received content, account and necessary connection information under its [privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/), applicable terms and your settings. This app makes no promise that the provider will never retain, train on or immediately delete content. Requests disable server conversation storage; that does not imply zero retention throughout the provider's systems. Share only material you may disclose, taking care with confidential and personal information.

**No tracking.** No advertising, advertising identifier, usage analytics or behavior-tracking code is included. Page Sprite's pointer tracking and animation run locally; pointer positions and animation timing do not leave the device. Platform and service providers may handle necessary network, security or store information under their own policies.

**Earlier editions.** Versions 1.0.x use user-supplied Gemini or Deepseek API keys. When their AI features are used, document content, questions and supported attached images go directly to the selected provider, authenticated with the corresponding key. Keys and preferences are stored locally. See [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Deepseek](https://www.deepseek.com/privacy) policies. Local storage does not mean that a key is never sent for authentication.

**Contact and changes.** Privacy contact: whkygl@163.com. Changes will update the date on this page. Do not send tokens, passwords or private PDFs through public issues or email.
